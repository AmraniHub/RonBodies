Frequently Asked Questions

Once your order is shipped, you'll receive an email with a tracking number and a link to track your package in real-time. You can also enter your order number on our Track Order page anytime.

We offer a 30-day hassle-free exchange policy for unused items in original packaging. If you received the wrong size or a defective item, we'll cover the return shipping cost. Simply contact our support team to initiate the process.

Our products use premium Medical Colombian compression technology — the gold standard in shapewear. Unlike cheaper alternatives, our fabric is breathable, anti-bacterial, and designed to provide targeted compression exactly where you need it, without rolling down or creating bulges.

We recommend measuring your natural waist and hips and comparing with our size chart on each product page. If you're between sizes, size up for comfort or size down for maximum compression. Our 24/7 support team is always available to help you find your perfect fit.

Standard delivery within the UAE takes 2-4 business days. Express shipping (1-2 business days) is available at checkout. We also ship internationally — delivery times vary by location. Free shipping applies on all UAE orders over AED 200.
